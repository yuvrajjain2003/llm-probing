How much survives when difficulty is removed

GPQA is the only one of the three where removing difficulty erases the entropy signal (0.289 falls to 0.025, not significant). MMLU-Pro keeps 0.200 of 0.410, and GlobalOpinionQA keeps 0.09 to 0.11 of 0.155 on the normalised target, both significantly above zero. Remaining length barely moves on any of the three.

Row set, in case he spots it: GPQA reads 0.289 here but 0.309 on the frontier slides. Both are correct. The frontier slides use the 1,178-row in-context subsample so that the probe and the frontier model are scored on identical rows; this slide uses the full sweep so that GPQA is comparable with MMLU-Pro and GlobalOpinionQA. Same for prefix length on remaining length, 0.552 there against 0.485 on the log-scale slide. Important: the two measures are a directed bracket only on GPQA and MMLU-Pro, where the label-derived measure is p_gold. GlobalOpinionQA has no gold answer, so its measures are the 0 percent cut normalised entropy and a text prediction of it, and the ordering flips between targets. Treat its two values as a range, not as bounds. Method, now that it is off the figure: difficulty is regressed out as a quadratic from BOTH the target and the prediction, within each cut point, refitted inside each of 2,000 question-bootstrap draws. The partialled number is NOT a subset of the published one, because partialling changes the denominator as well, so the markers on a row are not a decomposition of the grey circle.
