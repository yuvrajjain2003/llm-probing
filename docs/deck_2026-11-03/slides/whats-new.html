What is new since the last meeting

  • Two new datasets collected and verified. MMLU-Pro (2,500 questions) and GlobalOpinionQA (2,555), against GPQA's 198.
  • The probe reference rerun on both. MMLU-Pro beats GPQA on all five targets.
  • The frontier model comparison rerun on both, with confidence intervals. Its tie with our probe does not survive.
  • Question difficulty isolated as a confounder on GPQA and MMLU-Pro, and the result does not replicate the way we expected.
  • The length targets rechecked on a log scale. This overturns the standing objection that we are only counting tokens.
Everything here is scored within each cut point and then averaged, never pooled across depth. All intervals are 95% question-level bootstrap, 2,000 draws.
