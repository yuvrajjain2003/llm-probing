Removing difficulty makes our lead over text bigger, not smaller

Our probe minus TF-IDF on prefix, entropy, before and after difficulty is removed. MMLU-Pro +0.055 to +0.063 and GlobalOpinionQA +0.036 to +0.049, both significant and both widening. Only on GPQA does the advantage vanish.

This is the single strongest argument that the probe is not merely a difficulty reader. If difficulty were the whole story, removing it should erase our advantage over the words, and on the two large datasets it does the opposite. Signs here were checked against the source CSVs directly; a report in the repo prints them inverted. Method, now that it is off the figure: paired question-bootstrap difference, 2,000 draws, our probe minus TF-IDF on the same prefix, scored within each cut point and averaged. A filled marker means the 95% interval excludes zero. Do not read the growth as the probe improving: partialling changes the denominator for both approaches, so what this says is that the part of the signal which is NOT difficulty is where activations beat the words by most.
