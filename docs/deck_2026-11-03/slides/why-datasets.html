Why these two datasets

  • Our own allocation result said so. At a fixed rollout budget, many questions with few chains each beats few questions with many chains. 150 questions x 2 chains scored about the same as 30 questions x 25 chains. A new question is worth roughly 3.5x a new chain.
  • GPQA was too small to settle anything. 198 questions gave intervals of about plus or minus 0.06 R squared, and the comparisons we care about are differences of 0.02 to 0.05, so they sat inside the noise.
  • MMLU-Pro: objective, same shape, 12.6x the questions. Multiple choice with a gold answer, like GPQA. Isolates one variable: were our findings power-limited?
  • GlobalOpinionQA: subjective, no gold answer at all. Opinion questions with no correct response. Tests whether the probe needs correctness to work, or only needs the answer distribution to be concentrated.
The allocation result is round 4, idea 1. GlobalOpinionQA matters because p_gold is null on all 63,684 of its label rows, so every accuracy-based target and our original difficulty measure are simply unavailable there. Commitment still works, because it measures concentration of the answer distribution, not correctness.
