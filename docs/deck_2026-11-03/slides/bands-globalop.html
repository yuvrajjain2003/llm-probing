GlobalOpinionQA: no collapse at all, and we lead in every band

Entropy holds between 0.13 and 0.29 against 0.24 overall, with no collapse, and we are above the frontier model in all five bands. Remaining length runs 0.31 to 0.55 against 0.45 overall, ahead in all five bands and significant in all five.

This dataset has no gold answer, so the bands cannot be the share of correct rollouts. They are quintiles of the out-of-fold question-text difficulty measure instead, which is non-circular and comes out exactly equal-sized with no arbitrary tie-breaking. It is also the cleanest of the three in another way: the truth's spread inside each band stays between 0.80 and 1.10 of the overall spread, so no band is degenerate the way GPQA's very easy and MMLU-Pro's easy bands are. Entropy, ours against the frontier model: 0.269 vs 0.200, 0.285 vs 0.165, 0.203 vs 0.156, 0.129 vs 0.036, 0.196 vs 0.104. Remaining length differences are -0.297, -0.152, -0.308, -0.293 and -0.225 in our favour, every one significant. Note prefix length is near zero on entropy here, 0.01 to 0.07, so this dataset has none of GPQA's trivial-scalar problem. Method: questions are split into five difficulty bands and every approach is rescored inside each band, on identical rows, within each cut point and then averaged. Bars are 95% question bootstrap intervals, 2,000 draws. The top row is OFFSET CORRECTED: the stored predictions come from a fit on all questions and were never recentred per band, so an uncorrected band score is charged for an offset the full-set fit never carried. Recentring costs one degree of freedom and leaves the slope alone, so under-confidence is still penalised. The bottom row is the within-cut correlation, which has no offset at all and is the most forgiving view. Intervals inside a band are wide by construction, roughly 100 questions each, so it is the agreement across all five bands that carries the result, not any single band. Rows are the in-context row set, one chain per question, so every approach including the frontier model is scored on exactly the same rows.
