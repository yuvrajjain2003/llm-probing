The real structure is a depth effect, and averaging hid it

At the shallowest prefix almost all of the entropy signal is question difficulty. At the deepest, most of it is not. Partialled entropy runs -0.185 up to +0.142 on GPQA and 0.087 up to 0.272 on MMLU-Pro. Earlier rounds averaged over depth and so reported only the shallow story.

This is the cleanest new result and it is on both datasets. Mechanism is intuitive: at 10 percent of the chain there is almost no chain to read, so the activation is mostly a representation of the question. By 90 percent the chain itself carries information that difficulty cannot explain. Known limitation stated on the figure: there are no per-cut intervals here, because the stored bootstrap covers only the mean over cuts, so read shapes and not small differences. Method, now that it is off the figure: our probe, scored within each cut point. The orange line is an in-sample fit, so it is an upper limit on difficulty's own share and is not comparable to the other two lines. For scale, the mean-over-cuts interval half-width on entropy is 0.026 on MMLU-Pro, 0.066 on GPQA and 0.008 on GlobalOpinionQA.
