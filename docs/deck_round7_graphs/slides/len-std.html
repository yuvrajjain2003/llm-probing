Spread of remaining length, the same view

New and worth noting: on the log scale this target RISES with depth on all three datasets, where the raw version decays or is flat.

GPQA 0.356 to 0.459, MMLU-Pro 0.637 to 0.697, GlobalOpinionQA 0.301 to 0.555, all on log1p across the 10 to 90 percent cuts. This matters more than the other panels because the spread of remaining length is the target with the weakest claim to being readable from the prompt alone, and so the sharpest novelty claim in the related work. It is also the target that most needs the prefix: only 24 to 70 percent of its performance is available at the 0 percent cut, against 67 to 92 percent for the others.
