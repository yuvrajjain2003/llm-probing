GlobalOpinionQA: error inside each difficulty band

The cleanest of the three. The probe beats the band average in all five bands, and no band is degenerate.

GlobalOpinionQA has no gold answer, so it cannot band on p_gold. It bands on qtext_ent_norm_mean, an out-of-fold prediction of the question's mean normalised entropy from the question words alone, which is non-circular and gives exactly equal-sized quintiles. This dataset has no degenerate band: the truth's spread stays between 0.80 and 1.10 of the overall spread in every band, which is why it is the most trustworthy of the three panels.
