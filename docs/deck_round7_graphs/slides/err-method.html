Accuracy against error, with the buckets dropped

  • x axis is the question's accuracy, the share of its attempts that were correct. No banding.
  • y axis is per-question error: squared error for entropy, root squared error in tokens for length.
  • Both predictors are shown: our probe and the black box monitor, GPT-6 Luna zero-shot, raw and calibrated.
  • Your expectation holds. On MMLU-Pro, entropy error runs 0.387, 0.412, 0.403, 0.357, 0.250, 0.160 as accuracy goes 0 to 1.
  • The qualifier: the bin's own average runs 0.536 to 0.095 over the same range, so the probe wins 5 of 6 bins and loses only where every attempt was correct.
Both row sets are drawn and labelled on the figure: the ICL rows, which are the only rows the monitor was run on, and the full sweep rows, which have no monitor but 5 times the data. The number above each point is that bin's question count, so thin bins are visible rather than hidden.
