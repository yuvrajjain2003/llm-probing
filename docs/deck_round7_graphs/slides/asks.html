The three asks, and the short answer to each

  • MSE per difficulty band. The fix works. The two bands that read R squared of about minus 1 turn out to have the lowest absolute error of any band. R squared inside a band divides by that band's own variance, and in those bands the truth is nearly constant.
  • Accuracy against error, continuously. Your expectation holds in absolute error: error falls as accuracy rises. What survives of the shrinkage story is narrower: easy questions are the only place where guessing the bin average beats the probe.
  • Remaining length against checkpoint. On the raw scale, R squared decays with depth and prefix length alone matches us early. On the log scale we are ahead at every one of the 15 cuts across all three datasets, and the spread target rises with depth.
