How strong a baseline can we find?

  • Our probe, plus the two text baselines: word counts and prefix length
  • 4 other models: Qwen2.5-Instruct, Qwen2.5-Math, OLMo-3-7B, Mistral-7B
  • 5 sentence embedders: Qwen3-Embedding-8B, F2LLM-v2-8B, UltraBERT, BGE-M3, Granite
  • 7 probe types: two MLP sizes, two random forests, gradient boosting, two PCA variants
  • 3 layer choices: layer 14, layers 13 to 15, layers 20 to 22
  • All 22 on the same rows, same folds, same error bars
  • Strongest baseline: another model's activations. OLMo-3 ties us on all five
  • OLMo-3 and Mistral share no lineage with our model. Embedders and text are far behind
This is the real answer to "what is a strong baseline". Not the text baselines: another language model reading the same text. Two of the four are Qwen relatives, but OLMo-3 and Mistral are unrelated, and OLMo-3 still ties us. That is the uncomfortable part, and it connects to the last result in the deck.
